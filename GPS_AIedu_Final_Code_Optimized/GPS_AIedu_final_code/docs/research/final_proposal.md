# Research Proposal (Final v1.0)

## Title
Using the G.P.S. learning model and G.U.I.D.E. teaching process to improve Grade 11 mathematics learning with generative AI support.

## Background
Generative AI tools can provide fast explanations and feedback, but students often overuse them to obtain answers without thinking. Teachers also lack a clear framework for guiding AI use in class. This study proposes a structured model for student-AI interaction (G.P.S.) and a teacher workflow (G.U.I.D.E.).

## Objectives
1. Standardize the G.P.S. model for student learning.
2. Define a G.U.I.D.E. process for teacher monitoring and intervention.
3. Evaluate whether structured AI use improves outcomes compared to free use.

## Research Questions
1. How can the G.P.S. model be designed for Grade 11 math learning?
2. How does the G.U.I.D.E. process support teachers in managing AI use?
3. Can interaction data reveal distinct learning behavior patterns?
4. Does structured AI use outperform unrestricted AI use?

## Methodology
- Participants: 90 Grade 11 students.
- Groups: 60 students in the experimental group (G.P.S.) and 30 in the control group (free use).
- Duration: 6 weeks.
- Quantitative analysis: ANCOVA on pre/post assessments.
- Behavioral analysis: Markov Chain for step transitions and K-means clustering.

## Expected Outcomes
- Higher learning performance in the G.P.S. group.
- More consistent self-regulated learning behaviors.
- Practical guidance for safe, effective AI integration in schools.
